About this site

I studied Applied Mathematics and Quantitative Sciences at Emory University and am currently studying Computer Science at the University of Chicago. My interests are around data, programming, and practical problem-solving, especially projects that turn messy workflows into something clearer, faster, or easier to use.

What I’m exploring

I’m currently exploring SQL and Python utilities, dashboard ideas, automation workflows, and AI-assisted prototypes. I’m especially interested in small practical tools that solve real problems, even if they start as simple or unfinished experiments.

How I like to build

I like learning by making things. I use AI as a coding partner to test ideas faster, but I still care about understanding the logic, structure, and decisions behind each project.
